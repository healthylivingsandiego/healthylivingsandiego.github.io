Activity

Join a hiking group

San Diego Day Hikers is one of the region's longest-running groups, with easy-to-moderate outings roughly once a month plus occasional weekday evening hikes. UCSD's hiking club (open to students and staff) and several county YMCA branches also run regularly scheduled group hikes and walks for various fitness levels.

Where to go

Mission Trails Regional Park and its Cowles Mountain loop are among the most popular routes in the county. For a coastal option, Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve offers a few miles of bluff-top trails overlooking the Pacific, ranging from moderate to difficult.

Find a trail near you

Beyond the well-known spots, the wider county has well over 200 mapped trails covering a range of distances and difficulty levels — useful for finding something closer to home.
